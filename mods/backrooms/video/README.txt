Put BK_INTRO.mp4 in this folder.

It plays full screen when you press Enter on the home screen, and any key
skips it. H.264 in an .mp4 is what the engine can decode (that is what most
editors and phones produce); HEVC/H.265 is not, and is simply skipped.

Sound: an mp4's audio is usually AAC, which the engine cannot decode, so the
intro plays silent unless you drop BK_INTRO.ogg in beside it - that is played
alongside the video.

With no video here, pressing Enter just starts the level.
